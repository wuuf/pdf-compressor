For Android. In English and German.

What the app does

Fully offline

The app has no internet access. Your PDFs are processed only on your device.

No data, no tracking

No account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reports. No data is collected.

Handles large scans

Pages are processed one at a time, so even PDFs of hundreds of megabytes can be shrunk.

Never larger than before

If compressing doesn't help, the app keeps your original unchanged and tells you so.

Save or share

Save the result wherever you like, or share it straight to email, messengers and more.

Open source

The source code is freely available under the Apache License 2.0.

How it works

  1. Choose a PDF – using your device's file picker.
  2. Pick a level – Light, Recommended or Strong.
  3. Compress – then save or share the result.

Compression levels

LevelResolutionGood for
Light150 DPIHigh quality, e.g. for printing
Recommended110 DPIGood balance of quality and size
Strong72 DPISmallest file, e.g. for email attachments

Note: the app turns every page into an image. Text in the compressed PDF can no longer be selected or searched.
