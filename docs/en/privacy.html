Privacy policy

for the Android app “PDF Compressor” · Last updated: 25 November 2026

In short: The app does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. It has no internet access. Your PDF files are processed only on your device and only leave it when you save or share them yourself.

This is a translation. In case of doubt, the German version applies.

2. Processing on your device

When you choose a PDF file, the app reads it, turns each page into a compressed image and creates a new, smaller PDF file from it. This happens entirely on your device.

In doing so, the app stores the following files in its own cache, which other apps cannot access:

  • a working copy of the PDF file you chose,
  • the compressed PDF file.

These files stay on your device until Android clears the cache, you clear it in the app settings, or you uninstall the app. They are not transferred to us or to third parties.

3. No internet connection

The app does not request permission to access the internet and therefore cannot send or receive any data over the internet.

4. Permissions and file access

The app does not request general access to your storage. It opens and saves files only through the Android system file picker. This gives it access only to the file you choose there and to the location you choose for saving.

5. Saving and sharing

When you use “Save”, the result is stored in the location you choose. This may also be a cloud storage service if you pick one in the file picker.

When you use “Share”, the app hands the file to the app you choose in the Android share menu (for example an email app or a messenger). From then on, that app or its provider is responsible for further processing, and its privacy policy applies.

6. No tracking, no ads

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking services, no crash reporting and no user accounts. No usage data is collected.

7. Getting the app

If you download the app from an app store (for example Google Play), the store operator processes data under its own responsibility, such as data about your account and the download. We have no influence on this. The store's privacy policy applies.

8. This website

This website is hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany. When you visit the pages, your browser automatically sends technical data to the server. This includes your IP address, the date and time of access, the page requested, the amount of data transferred, the previously visited page (referrer), and your browser type and operating system. This data may be stored in server log files.

This processing is necessary to deliver the website and keep it secure and stable. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in doing so (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Log data is deleted as soon as it is no longer needed for these purposes. It is not combined with other data or used to analyse individual people. See Hetzner's privacy policy for more information.

The website itself sets no cookies, loads no external fonts or scripts and uses no analytics.

9. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). Since the app itself processes no personal data, we generally hold no data about you. You can contact us at the address above at any time.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).

10. Changes

We update this privacy policy when the app or legal requirements change. The version published here always applies.
